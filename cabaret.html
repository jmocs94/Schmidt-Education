🎭 DAHS Cabaret Night 2026 — Track Player

Backing track cue player. Nothing auto-advances — you're always in control of when the next number starts.

ℹ How to use (click to collapse)
  • Space or P — Play / Pause
  • N or → — Next track
  • B or ← — Previous track
  • M — Mute / unmute (bonus shortcut)
  • Click a track once to load it (paused); double-click to load and play immediately.
  • Loading a track interrupts whatever is currently playing — there's only one "live" track at a time, same as a real cue player.
  • Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder the setlist. Use ✖ to drop a number from tonight's run, or "Add Local File" / the emergency YouTube box to bring in something new.
  • "Keep screen awake" stops the booth laptop/Chromebook from locking mid-show — leave it checked.
  • Tracks preload into the browser's memory as soon as this page opens. Once a track shows ✓ cached, it'll keep playing even if the venue's Wi-Fi drops. Keep this tab open for the whole show — reloading the page clears the cache and starts the download over.
